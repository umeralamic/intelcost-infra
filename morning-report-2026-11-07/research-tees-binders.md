# Research: water main tees (material) and asphalt binder premiums

Session: 2026-11-04 (for the 2026-11-07 morning report). Every figure below came from a page or PDF that was
opened and read in this session. Search snippets that were not opened are not used. No cost-data publisher
and no Ferguson.com page was used. (Ferguson Waterworks shows up below only as a bidder named in public bid
tabulations.)

Grades: **A** = several public bids that agree. **B** = one public bid or contract, or a distributor list.
**C** = a single quote, or data more than 3 years old. **D** = retail x 0.50.

### B.4 Excluded or not opened

- **Arkansas DOT weighted average unit prices, 2025-08-13 to 2026-08-12**
  (https://media.ark.org/ardot/Aug-12-2026-Weighted-Average-Prices.pdf): opened. Arkansas pays asphalt binder
  as its own item by grade (A.B. PG64-22 / PG70-22 / PG76-22, $/ton of binder). In this report the low bid on
  every binder item is $120/ton, and the weighted averages run in the wrong order (surface 1/2 in: PG64-22
  $271.13, PG70-22 $154.06, PG76-22 $256.43). Contractors unbalance the binder against the aggregate, so these
  are **not used**.
- **Louisiana DOTD price indices** (PG 64-22, 70-22M, 76-22M): blocked by Cloudflare (403), not opened.
- **Arkansas DOT Asphalt Binder Price Index** (PG 64-22, 70, 76): the page loads, but the values sit in an
