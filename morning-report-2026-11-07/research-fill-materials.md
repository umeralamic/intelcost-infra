# Fill and liner materials: price research

Researched 2026-11-04. US prices only. Every figure below comes from a document that was opened and read. No figure is taken from a search snippet. No cost-data publishers were used, and no Ferguson pages.

**Method, in order of preference:**
1. Public bids and contracts: bid tabulations, awarded unit prices and government price data.
2. Distributor price lists.
3. Retail prices. These are recorded at 50% of the shelf price and graded D.

**Grades:**
- **A:** several public bids or contracts that agree.
- **B:** one public bid or contract, or a distributor list.
- **C:** a single distributor quote, older public data (over 3 years), or public data that is not a transaction price.
- **D:** retail price × 0.50.

**Conversions used:**
- 1 SY = 9 SF.
- 1 metric ton = 1.10231 short tons.
- Loose density is assumed only where a source prices by the CY: sand 1.35 tons/CY, clay 1.30 tons/CY. These are stated assumptions, not source figures.

**Engineer's estimates.** These appear in some tabs. They are listed for context but left out of every median.
