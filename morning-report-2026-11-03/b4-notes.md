## Temporary chain link: term price
I found no public bid with an all-in term price per LF (delivery, install and removal included).

The nearest public figure is the San Antonio award (IFB 5341, opened 12/20/2023, KKH Rentals):
- Rent is monthly: $720/mo for 144 LF and $2,000/mo for 400 LF, both $5.00/LF-month.
- Installation is a one-time $500 and removal a one-time $500.
- A 3-month term on 400 LF works out to (3×2000 + 1000)/400 = **$17.50/LF**.
- That figure is my arithmetic, not a quoted term price.

A 2026 rental marketplace guide lists 6 ft chain link at $8–22/LF for a 3-month term and $30–80/LF for 12 months, with setup billed separately ($200–1,000). It is marketing content, not a bid.

## Caveats
- Zoro and Kuhlman are treated as distributor lists, so no 0.50 factor was applied. Their single-unit prices may run higher than yard or contract pricing.
- The Gwinnett multi-use-path award (BL069-25, 2025) shows **installed** prices only. I did not use them for material keys. For reference: silt fence Type A $1.48–4.25/LF, 4 ft orange barrier fence $1.50–4.88/LF, 6 ft 9 ga chain link $28–50/LF.
- Several state and county PDFs and fence-distributor sites refused automated fetches (403/429). Those were skipped and none of their figures are used.
