# B5: published light-pole foundation standard (read from the drawing)

## Source

- **Agency:** Indiana Department of Transportation (INDOT), Standard Drawings, September 2025 book, 800E series
- **Drawing:** Standard Drawing No. **E 807-LTFD-05**, "LIGHT FOUNDATION" (square and round foundation details)
- **Date / revision:** sheet dated SEPTEMBER 2002, revision -05, signed 9-03-02 (Design Standards Engineer, Chief Highway Engineer). Still carried in the Sep 2025 book.
- **URL:** https://secure.in.gov/dot/div/contracts/standards/drawings/sep25/800e/e800%20combined%20pdfs/E807-LTFD.pdf
- **Companion sheet (anchor bolts and pole sizes):** Standard Drawing No. **E 807-LTST-02**, "ALUMINUM LIGHT POLE WITH TRANSFORMER BASE", SEPTEMBER 2012, signed 09/04/12.
  URL: https://secure.in.gov/dot/div/contracts/standards/drawings/sep25/800e/e800%20combined%20pdfs/E807-LTST.pdf
- **Pole it carries:** E 807-LTST-02 tabulates roadway poles of E.M.H. (effective mounting height) 25, 30, 35, 40 and 45 ft with 5 to 25 ft arms, on a transformer base. E 807-LTFD-05 is one foundation for all of them (no per-height table). This fits the 25-40 ft target.

How read: both PDFs were downloaded and their text layer extracted (pdftotext -layout). The sheet graphics could not be rendered here, so a value that exists only as drawn linework (for example the bolt count in plan) is reported as not shown.

## Flagpole foundation standard

**Not found.** No US state, city or federal standard drawing giving a flagpole foundation's diameter, depth and reinforcement could be located and opened in this session. What turned up instead:

- VA spec section 10 75 00 Flagpoles (https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/VA/VANCAS/VANCA107500.pdf): a specification, not a drawing. It says to size the foundation for the site's wind loads and gives no dimensions or bars.
- VicRoads (Australia) STD S0062 "Flag pole footing details": not a US standard, and it covers a bus-stop flag pole, so it is not used here.
- Manufacturer footing tables, for example a 20 ft pole on a 2'-6" deep x 30" diameter footing: these come from a vendor, not a public standard, so they are excluded.

No flagpole numbers are given here because none were read from a public standard.
