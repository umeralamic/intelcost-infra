## 3. Asphalt binder premiums over PG 64-22

**PG 58-28, PG 64-28, PG 70-22 premiums: not found.** No public index that prices these grades alongside PG 64-22 could be opened.

What was tried and why it failed:
- Kansas DOT Monthly Asphalt Material Index, Louisiana DOTD price indices, Tennessee DOT PG70-22 / PG64-22 index PDFs,
  FDOT, Flathead County MT liquid-asphalt contract: all returned HTTP 403 / Cloudflare challenge. Search-engine snippets
  (not opened, so not used): Flathead County lists PG64-22 $522.90 vs PG58-28 $537.90 (Calumet) and $580.00 vs $575.00
  (Idaho Asphalt Supply), undated; Louisiana Jul-2025 PG 67-22 $580/ton vs PG 70-22M $687/ton.
- Indiana DOT PG binder index, Oregon DOT MACMP, Mississippi DOT: single-grade or no target grades.
- Oregon bid data: binder is bid per ton by grade but nearly always at a nominal $0-$1 (paid through the index), so unusable.
  One contract (15616, 2025) bid PG 64-28 $750/ton and PG 70-28ER $815/ton in the same job (neither is a target grade pair).
- SDDOT: PG 58-34 $715.52, PG 64-34 $800.49 (2024); PG 64-28 $984.38 vs PG 58-34 $906.91 (2023, 1 bid). No PG 64-22 line.
- Arkansas DOT weighted averages (A.B. PG64-22 / PG70-22 / PG76-22 binder items): nominal bids, PG 70-22 averaged below PG 64-22; not usable.

Data point for a grade not asked for (polymer PG 76-22): MDOT-MS Nov 2025: PG-64-22 $564.00/ton, PG-67-22 $574.09, PG-76-22 $720.00
(derived premium 76-22 over 64-22 = $720.00 - $564.00 = **$156.00 / ton binder**).
